STI Slips 0.67% to 5,574.69 as SATS Leads Gains and Jardine Matheson Paces Declines

Wednesday, 5 August 2026 · Longbridge Singapore

The Straits Times Index closed at 5,574.69 on Wednesday, down 37.56 points, or 0.67%, after trading between 5,568.48 and 5,614.84 during the session. Advancers narrowly outnumbered decliners across the benchmark's 30 constituents, 13 higher against 12 lower and five unchanged, with SATS leading the session's gains and Jardine Matheson leading the losses.

The index opened down about 0.2% at 5,601.09, with gainers still outnumbering decliners 124 to 61 across the broader market in early trade. The decline widened through the session as Singapore's three local banks fell together for the first time in several sessions of split moves: DBS declined 1.28%, UOB 0.44% and OCBC 0.31%. Singtel eased 1.81% and Jardine Matheson dropped 2.37%, and the combined weight of these large, closely tracked counters outpaced gains spread across the rest of the STI basket.

Session Movers

SATS (S58.SG, +2.77%) led Wednesday's gainers after Changi Airport Group and SATS jointly deployed autonomous wheelchairs at Terminals 2 and 3, automating routine passenger transport following a 20% rise in assistance requests between 2023 and 2025 and more than 13,000 passengers served during trials. The rollout adds an operational-efficiency angle to SATS's ground-handling business and comes a day after the stock fell 2.29% on August 4, with the rebound also arriving ahead of a first-quarter business update due August 19 after market close. SATS trades at 2.54 times book, near the middle of its ten-year range, with a consensus Strong Buy rating.

Jardine Matheson (J36.SG, -2.37%) led the session's declines, its worst day among STI constituents. The move follows Tuesday's disclosure that the group is ending its 44-year Oxbridge undergraduate scholarship programme, redirecting the funding toward universities in Asia, and comes despite Jardine Matheson directors having bought about US$995,000 of shares on August 3 — a purchase that did not stem Wednesday's weakness. The stock trades at 0.67 times book, above its own five-year range though still below the industry median of 1.04 times, with a consensus Strong Buy rating and a target price implying about 34% upside.

One point worth noting

Advancers outnumbered decliners 13 to 12 among the STI's 30 constituents on Wednesday, a break from recent sessions when the basket's breadth and the index's direction moved in step; this time the index still fell 0.67%. The gap traced to the banks, Jardine Matheson and Singtel — the same heavyweights behind the session's decline — whose combined weight outpaced gains spread across the remaining 25 constituents, 13 of which rose.

STI收跌0.67%报5,574.69点，SATS领涨、怡和洋行领跌

2026年8月5日 星期三 · 长桥新加坡

海峡时报指数周三收报5,574.69点，跌37.56点，跌幅0.67%，全天交投区间5,568.48至5,614.84点。30只成分股中13只上涨、12只下跌、5只持平，涨家数略多于跌家数；SATS领涨，怡和洋行领跌。

指数早盘低开约0.2%，报5,601.09点，彼时全市场上涨家数以124比61领先下跌家数。跌势在盘中扩大，三大银行罕见同向下跌——此前数个交易日三行走势持续分化，周三星展跌1.28%、大华跌0.44%、华侨银行跌0.31%，为近期首次同步下行。新加坡电信跌1.81%，怡和洋行跌2.37%，这几只权重较大、市场关注度高的个股跌幅叠加，抵消了成分股中其余个股的涨幅。

个股焦点

SATS（S58.SG，+2.77%）周三全场领涨，此前樟宜机场集团与SATS联合在2号、3号航站楼部署自动驾驶轮椅，用于协助行动不便旅客，此举针对2023至2025年间协助需求激增20%的情况，试运行阶段已服务超过1.3万人次旅客。该项目为SATS的地勤业务增添了运营效率方面的看点，且股价在8月4日下跌2.29%后出现反弹，同时公司将于8月19日盘后发布截至6月30日止季度的首季业务更新。该股目前市净率2.54倍，处于近十年区间中段，分析师共识评级为「强力买入」。

怡和洋行（Jardine Matheson，J36.SG，-2.37%）周三全场跌幅最深，为当天成分股中表现最差的一只。此前一日（8月4日）该集团宣布终止长达44年的牛津剑桥本科奖学金计划，转而将资金投向亚洲地区的高校；同时，8月3日怡和洋行董事曾购入约99.5万美元公司股份，但这一增持并未阻止周三的股价走弱。该股目前市净率0.67倍，高于自身五年区间，但仍低于1.04倍的行业中位数，分析师共识评级为「强力买入」，目标价隐含约34%上行空间。

一个值得注意的点

周三STI的30只成分股中，上涨家数（13只）多于下跌家数（12只），指数却仍下跌0.67%——此前数个交易日，成分股广度与指数方向大体同步，这次出现了背离。差距集中在银行股、怡和洋行与新加坡电信这几只当天领跌的权重股上，它们的合计跌幅抵消了其余25只成分股（其中13只上涨）分散贡献的涨幅。
